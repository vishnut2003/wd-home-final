First Step Day 1–3

First Consultation

Book a confirmed slot with a senior Technical Consultant — studio, site, or virtual. We listen to your vision, plot details, family needs and budget comfort. No obligation, no advance.

What you receive

  • Confirmed appointment
  • Meeting summary & notes
  • Package overview brochure
  • Indicative cost range

What happens

  • TC listens — vision, plot & family needs
  • Feasibility & by-laws discussed
  • Budget & timeline walkthrough
  • No sales pressure
2
02
Pre-Engagement Day 4–10

Slot Booking & Conceptual Design

Our design team prepares a complete conceptual package — floor plans, costing, facade & 3D walkthrough — purpose-built for your plot, presented at a dedicated design review session.

What you receive

  • Conceptual floor plan (all floors, by-law compliant)
  • Area bifurcation sheet (FAR, covered, open)
  • Package-wise costing with area breakdown
  • Front facade concept presentation
  • Interior 3D conceptual walkthrough
  • WD Meeting Kit

What happens

  • Design team prepares plans from your brief
  • By-law & area optimisation reviewed
  • Costing computed with area-wise breakup
  • Full package presented at design review
3
03
Commitment Week 1–2

Contract & Project Kickoff

Scope, specs, milestone-linked payments and timelines — formalised in plain language. Nothing moves until you are confident and have signed.

What you receive

  • WD Project Contract (full scope & specs)
  • Itemised BOQ
  • Milestone-linked payment schedule
  • Project timeline & start date
  • Assigned Project Manager

What happens

  • Contract review session with TC
  • Soil testing ordered & scheduled
  • PM assigned — your single contact
  • CRM portal access granted
4
04
Design Phase A Week 3–8

Schematic Design & Approvals

Architects develop floor plans, elevation concepts and structural scheme from soil test data. Statutory approvals and regulatory submissions run in parallel.

What you receive

  • Schematic floor plans (all floors)
  • Elevation concepts
  • Soil test report (3rd party)
  • Building plan approval & NOCs

What happens

  • Architect briefing & site measurement
  • Soil test & foundation design
  • Up to 2 design revision rounds
  • Approval drawings submitted
5
05
Design Phase B Week 6–12

Design Development

Schematics evolve into construction-ready drawings. MEP, interiors, finishes and 3D visualisations locked before excavation begins — eliminating costly on-site changes.

What you receive

  • Working drawings — arch & structural
  • MEP layout drawings
  • 3D exterior & interior views
  • Finalized BOQ & spec sheet

What happens

  • 3D walkthrough review with client
  • Material & finish selection
  • MEP clash detection
  • Final design freeze & sign-off
6
06
Construction Months 3–14

Execution Phase

100% in-house team. Every milestone governed by 14-day micro-plans, every stage inspected against our 350+ point quality checklist. Real-time CCTV & client portal access.

What you receive

  • Weekly progress reports
  • Milestone certificates
  • Material MTC records
  • Live CCTV — 24/7 access

Construction milestones

  • Foundation & plinth → RCC structure
  • Brickwork, plastering & waterproofing
  • MEP rough-in (electrical, plumbing)
  • Flooring, false ceiling & finishing
7
07
Completion Final week

Handover

Internal snag audit, every defect corrected, then room-by-room walkthrough with you. Keys and the complete Completion Kit handed over only after your sign-off.

Completion Kit

  • 3rd Party Structural Certificate
  • All Material MTC Reports
  • As-built drawings (arch & MEP)
  • Warranty cards (structure, waterproofing)
  • Snag-clearance certificate

What happens

  • Internal QC snag audit
  • All defects corrected first
  • Client walkthrough & room demo
  • Keys & Completion Kit handed over
8
08
Ongoing 6–24 months

Post-Handover Support

Moving in is not the end. Our in-house team stays invested in your home throughout the warranty period — electricians, plumbers, civil team, all available to you.

Support coverage

  • 3-month & 6-month site visits
  • Paint touch-up Year 1
  • Plumbing & electrical support
  • 48-hour priority response

By package

  • Atmos Blue Advance — 12 months
  • Avenue Beige — 18 months
  • Ambience Black — 24 months + PM
