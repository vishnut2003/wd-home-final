Schematic Design & Approvals

Architects develop floor plans, elevation concepts and structural scheme from soil test data. Statutory approvals and regulatory submissions run in parallel.

What you receive

Design Development

Schematics evolve into construction-ready drawings. MEP, interiors, finishes and 3D visualisations locked before excavation begins — eliminating costly on-site changes.

What you receive

  • Working drawings — arch & structural
  • MEP layout drawings
  • 3D exterior & interior views
  • Finalized BOQ & spec sheet

What happens

  • 3D walkthrough review with client
  • Material & finish selection
  • MEP clash detection
  • Final design freeze & sign-off
Construction Months 3–14

Execution Phase

100% in-house team. Every milestone governed by 14-day micro-plans, every stage inspected against our 350+ point quality checklist. Real-time CCTV & client portal access.

  • Milestone certificates
  • Material MTC records
  • Live CCTV — 24/7 access

Construction milestones

  • Foundation & plinth → RCC structure

Non-negotiable foundation

Backed by warranties. Certified by experts.

Every WD home comes with industry-leading warranties, independent third-party certifications and documented reports — handed to you at completion. No fine print, no exceptions.

25+ Yrs
Structural Warranty

RCC superstructure guaranteed for 25+ years — M25 grade, seismic Zone-IV compliant, earthquake-resistant framed structure.

10 Yrs
Waterproofing Warranty

3-layer premium waterproofing system with Tapecrete protection — Dr. Fixit / CICO / Sunanda grade, covering terraces, bathrooms and basements.

1–5 Yrs
Anti-Termite Warranty

Pre-construction and post-construction anti-termite treatment with a certified warranty — protecting your home's woodwork and foundation from the ground up.

3rd Party Structural Certificate

An independent structural engineer verifies and certifies your RCC frame at handover — a document that belongs to you, not us.

Soil Testing Report

Pre-construction soil bearing capacity test conducted by an accredited lab — foundation design is based on actual ground data, not assumptions.

Material MTC Reports

Mill Test Certificates (MTC) for TMT steel, cement and key structural materials — every batch documented and provided to you in your Completion Kit.
